How it works

  1. Choose the case

    Burnt winding takes rewinding at ELEC 8.18.9. Theft takes new copper at ELEC 10.3.2, full oil and a workshop quotation.

  2. Enter the rating

    KVA, HV and LV line voltages, winding connections, scheme name and location.

  3. Check the copper weight

    The calculation tab shows every step. Enter the nameplate weight if you have it; it overrides the calculated value.

  4. Download

    Excel workbook with all sheets, or print the cover, abstract, detailed estimate and calculation.

Calculation basis

Winding weight

  • Current I = KVA × 1000 / (√3 × V); delta phase current = I / √3
  • Volts per turn Et = K √KVA, K = 0.45 for 3-phase distribution
  • Core area from E = 4.44 f Bm Ai, Bm = 1.55 T (CRGO)
  • Conductor area = phase current / J, J = 2.5 A/mm²
  • Weight = 3 × turns × mean turn × area × 8890 kg/m³, + 5%

Rates

  • TG Common SoR 2026-27, Part-III Electrical, Sec. 8.18 and 10.3
  • Contractor’s profit & overheads 13.615% added to SoR basic rates
  • Workshop repair and CCTV by quotation, exclusive of GST
  • Labour cess 1%, NAC 0.1%, GST 18%, rounded up to the next ₹1,000

Limits

  • Calculated copper weight is approximate (±10–15%)
  • Some 8.18 rates change with the KVA band; check and edit them in the Data tab
  • Oil quantity is interpolated from typical values unless the nameplate value is entered
  • To be checked and approved by the competent engineer
