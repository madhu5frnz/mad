How to use this tool
  1. Choose the bridgeS.L.R.B. or D.L.R.B. at the top right.
  2. Enter the HPsQ, bed width, FSD, side slope (1.5 for 1.5:1), free board, bed fall (2500 for 1 in 2500), design CBL.
  3. Enter road & levelsFRL and ground level in metres, no + sign. Press use on the suggested span and foundation level.
  4. Check and downloadGreen means done. If red, press Auto design. Then download PDF, Excel or DXF from the tabs.

Sheet 1: plan and sectional elevation. Sheet 2: sections and reinforcement details. Use + and − to zoom.

Design to IRC 5, IRC 6, IRC 21 (WSM), IRC 78, IRC SP:13 and IS 456. Results must be checked and approved by the competent engineer before use. SBC, silt factor and backfill φ are assumptions until confirmed at site.
