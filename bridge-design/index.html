How it works

  1. Choose the structureS.L.R.B., D.L.R.B. or U.T. (drain under the canal) at the top.
  2. Enter the HPsQ, bed width, FSD, side slope (1.5 for 1.5:1), free board, bed fall (2500 for 1 in 2500), CBL.
  3. Site & design conditionsFRL, ground level, soil, vents; then code, grades, loading, edge and seismic zone for your site. Press use on suggestions.
  4. Check & downloadGreen means done; if red press Auto design. Download PDF, Excel or DXF.

Design basis

What you can set for your site
  • Hydraulic particulars, road and ground levels, bed material (silt factor), SBC, backfill φ and unit weight, friction
  • Number of vents (piers added automatically), clear span, skew, carriageway, footpaths
  • Deck edge: kerb with hand railing or RCC crash barrier
  • Design method (IRC 21 WSM or IRC 112 LSM), concrete and steel grades, exposure and cover
  • Live load classes, seismic zone and importance factor, floor protection
Codes applied
  • IRC 5:2015 carriageway, kerb, clearances
  • IRC 6:2017 Class A / B / 70R, impact, braking, footpath load, surcharge, seismic (cl.219)
  • IRC 21:2000 WSM deck, effective width (cl.305.16), permissible stresses
  • IRC 112:2020 LSM deck: flexure, shear, SLS stresses, minimum steel, cover
  • IRC 78:2014 scour (1.27 / 2.0 dsm), foundation depth, stability FOS
  • IRC SP:13:2004 small bridges, floor protection
  • IS 456, IS 1786, IS 2502; MORTH SD/202 railing, crash barrier, specifications
  • IS 7784 Pt 1 & 2 U.T.: Lacey waterway, fluming, transitions, Unwin barrel loss; Dicken's flood; CDO drop-wall rules
Scope and limits
  • RCC solid slab decks, simply supported, up to about 8–10 m per vent; wider canals use more vents
  • Plain concrete gravity abutments, piers and wing walls on open foundations; where they cannot work (high walls, poor soil, Zone IV–V) the tool says so
  • Up to two traffic lanes; skew above 20° needs separate skew-slab analysis
  • U.T.: single / multi-cell RCC box (closed-frame analysis, WSM) with vertical drop, cistern, splayed transitions and gravity head / wing / return walls
  • Results are a design aid. They must be checked and approved by the competent engineer; soil values must come from investigation

Inputs

Sheet 1: plan and sectional elevation. Sheet 2: sections and reinforcement details. Use + and − to zoom.
